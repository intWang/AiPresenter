# AiPresenter Persistent Memory

## Durable User Coaching

- Use English for RingCentral Video demos.
- Chinese narration sounded stiff in this project; do not default to it for RingCentral Video feature tours.
- Make feature-to-feature transitions natural. Avoid standalone transition pauses when the next action can carry the transition.
- Keep pauses short between related Meeting controls.
- Keep audio and UI actions synchronized. For menus, dialogs, and panels, open the control shortly after narration starts, keep it visible while explaining, and close it after the spoken explanation finishes.
- Cover Meeting features completely, especially top bar controls, people controls, media controls, collaboration controls, More actions, settings, notes, background, recording, and Leave.
- Close or handle foreground dialogs before continuing. A blocked window makes the tour look broken.
- Do not click destructive controls during a tour unless explicitly confirmed. Recording and Leave are explain-only by default.

## RingCentral Video Observations

- Report issue opens a foreground dialog and blocks other controls.
- Add coworkers and Invite open an invite dialog that blocks the toolbar.
- Notes opens a side panel with Start notes and Also record this meeting.
- Background and Settings open the Settings dialog.
- Share opens a picker and should not press the final Share button without confirmation.
- Raise hand is a toggle and should be restored after demonstration.
